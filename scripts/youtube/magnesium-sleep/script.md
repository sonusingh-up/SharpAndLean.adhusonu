## Sources I opened and checked today

- **S1.** NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, Magnesium fact sheet for health professionals (updated 6 January 2026): adult RDA 310 to 420 mg; 48% of Americans get less than recommended from food; upper limit 350 mg a day from supplements and medicines only, not food; aspartate, citrate, lactate and chloride absorbed better than oxide and sulfate; carbonate, chloride, gluconate and oxide most often cause diarrhoea; high doses cause diarrhoea, nausea and cramping; toxicity risk with impaired kidney function; separate from bisphosphonates by at least 2 hours and from tetracycline or quinolone antibiotics by 2 hours before or 4 to 6 hours after. https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Magnesium-HealthProfessional/
- **S2.** Mah and Pitre 2021, BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies: 3 randomised trials, 151 older adults; sleep onset 17.36 minutes shorter than placebo (p = 0.0006); total sleep time not significantly improved; evidence rated low to very low. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12906-021-03297-z
- **S3.** Schuster et al. 2025, Nature and Science of Sleep: 155 adults aged 18 to 65 with self-reported poor sleep; 250 mg elemental magnesium as bisglycinate daily; Insomnia Severity Index fell 3.9 points versus 2.3 on placebo at week 4 (p = 0.049); small effect (Cohen's d = 0.2); exploratory signal of larger benefit with lower dietary magnesium intake. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40918053/
