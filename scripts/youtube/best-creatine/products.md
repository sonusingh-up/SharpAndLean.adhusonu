## Things to re-check before publishing

- **NOW Sports prices.** The 8 oz and 21.2 oz sizes were on sale when read; the
  35.2 oz size showed $41.24 as both sale and regular price, so that is the
  pack used. The pack shot on screen is the 8 oz bottle (noted on screen).
- **BulkSupplements testing.** "None seen" means no sport certification was
  found on the product page or its data, not that none exists. The label's
  maintenance dose is 3 tsp (about 7 g) a day.
- **Optimum Nutrition.** Flavoured versions list a 6 g serving; the figures
  here are for the unflavoured powder.
- **Read but not used:** Myprotein (serving size and regular prices did not
  load), Momentous ($42.99 for 90 servings, NSF Certified for Sport) and Klean
  Athlete ($34.50 for 60 servings, NSF Certified for Sport).
