# Caption: vitamin D reel

Check your vitamin D label: micrograms and IU are not the same thing. ☀️

1 mcg = 40 IU
10 mcg = 400 IU
25 mcg = 1,000 IU

Mixing them up is the most common way people take far more than they meant to.

For most UK adults the advice is 10 mcg (400 IU) a day from October to March. The upper limit for adults is 100 mcg (4,000 IU) a day, and a plain vitamin D3 is all most people need.
