Fish oil benefits: what the research actually shows. 🐟

✅ Triglycerides: lowered by about 15% on average.
🤏 The heart: it may slightly reduce some events, but a trial of nearly 26,000 healthy adults found no significant drop in major heart events.
🏷️ Buying one? Compare the EPA and DHA, not the "fish oil 1,000 mg" on the front.

Side effects are usually mild: fishy burps and an upset stomach. Taking it with meals helps. Check with a doctor first if you take any medicine.
