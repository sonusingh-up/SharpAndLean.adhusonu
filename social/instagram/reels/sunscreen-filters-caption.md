# Reel caption: new vs old sunscreen filters

**Caption**

For 27 years, US sunscreens were stuck with the same old filters. In June 2026 the FDA finally added a new one: bemotrizinol.

Old (avobenzone): breaks down in sunlight, so it needs extra stabilisers.
New (bemotrizinol): UVA and UVB cover, stays stable in the sun, and very little passes through your skin.

The first three US sunscreens with it:
1. Ultra Violette Unblock Screen SPF 50
2. Neutrogena Ultra Sheer Daily Face SPF 70
3. Neutrogena Clear Face SPF 60

Older sunscreens still work, so keep wearing yours. We have not tested these three yet.
