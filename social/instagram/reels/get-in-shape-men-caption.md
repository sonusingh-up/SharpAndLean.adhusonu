Get in shape fast? You don't need a six-day split.

Start with two full-body strength sessions a week.
Add cardio you can recover from, like a 10–20 minute walk or cycle.
Pick one goal (lose fat, build muscle or feel fitter) and let your food match it.
Aim for at least 7 hours of sleep.
Treat 8 weeks as a review point, not a deadline for abs.
