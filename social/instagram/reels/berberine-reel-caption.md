Berberine benefits: what the research actually shows. 💊

✅ Weight: a 2022 review of 18 studies found a significant drop in weight and BMI.
⚠️ The small print: mainly above 1 g a day, for more than 8 weeks, and many of the studies were at high risk of bias.
🔬 Diabetes and heart disease risk factors: still being studied.
❌ It is not "nature's Ozempic". No supplement acts like the drug.

Side effects are mostly digestive: nausea, bloating, constipation or diarrhoea. Avoid it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, and never give it to infants. Check with a doctor if you take any medicine.

Buying one? The label should state berberine in milligrams, not the weight of a bark powder.
